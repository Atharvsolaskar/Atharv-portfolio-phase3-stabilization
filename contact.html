What’s your typical process for a new project?

I start by understanding what’s actually broken or needed, no long proposal decks. From there I build a working draft fast, you react to something real, and we refine together until it’s right.

How long does a project usually take?

Depends on scope. A focused website or tool can take 1 to 3 weeks. Larger builds, apps, systems, ongoing platforms, take longer, and I’ll give you a clear timeline before we start, not after.

Do you work with a fixed price list?

No. Every project is quoted based on what it actually needs. I’d rather understand your situation first than hand you a generic package.

Can I see examples of past work?

Yes, reach out and I’ll walk you through recent builds, including what worked and what I’d do differently, real talk, not just a highlight reel.

Do you offer ongoing support after launch, or just build and leave?

Ongoing support, real support. Most of what I build includes a maintenance option, and I stay reachable after launch, not gone the day you pay.
